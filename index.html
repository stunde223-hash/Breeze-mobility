02 / THE PEOPLE BEHIND BREEZE

EXPERIENCE
FROM THE STREET UP.

Built by someone who knows the work.

Our founder, Sean, started in bike rebalancing with Motivate, the operator of CitiBike in New York City, in 2015. In 2019, he moved into operations management at White Fox Scooters.

He launched his own micro-mobility business in 2021. What began as a scooter-sharing platform grew into Breeze’s micro EV fleet management service, built around the real needs of operators.
